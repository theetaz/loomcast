# Recipe: vertical explainer

A complete plan for a 30–60 s explainer short. Use it with the `scriptwriting`, `storyboarding`
and `captions-and-pacing` skills; the format's rules override any number here.

## Beat sheet

| Beat | Time | Words | Script | Card (tier, purpose) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0–3 s | ≤ 8 | The promise in the format's hook style | tier1 `section` or `stat` |
| Context | 3–10 s | 15–20 | Why it matters to the viewer | tier1 `overview` or tier2 `label` |
| Point 1 | 10–20 s | 20–25 | First reason, with an example | tier1 `stat` or tier2 `list` |
| Point 2 | 20–30 s | 20–25 | Second reason, with an example | a different purpose from point 1 |
| Point 3 | 30–40 s | 20–25 | Third reason or the "how" | tier1 `section` or tier2 `label` |
| Payoff | 40–50 s | 15–20 | The answer the hook promised | tier1 `section` |
| Call to action | last 3–5 s | ≤ 10 | One sentence, or empty | shares the payoff frame |

For a 30 s format, drop point 3 and shorten the context. For 60 s, give each point a second
sentence and, if a segment runs past its card's maximum, a second frame.

## Script checklist

- Five to seven segments: hook, context, two or three points, payoff (the CTA is the `cta`
  field).
- 75–150 words in total at about 150 words a minute.
- Each point names one concrete example: a command, a product behaviour, a number from the
  sources.
- `onScreenText` on every segment, under 40 characters.

## Storyboard checklist

- One frame per segment, split only when a segment is longer than its card's maximum.
- The first frame starts at 0 ms; the last ends at the voice's end.
- No card twice in a row; no more than two frames of the same purpose back to back.
- Slots filled within their limits; `<em>` on one phrase per headline at most.

## Review checklist

- The hook is understood with the sound off within 3 seconds (card plus first caption).
- Every number on a card appears in the sources.
- The payoff answers the hook.
- Nothing from the format's `avoid` list appears in the script, cards or caption.
